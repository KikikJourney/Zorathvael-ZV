What this dashboard does

  • Reads token supply and decimals directly from public RPC endpoints.
  • Optionally connects supported wallets after a button click.
  • Reads ZV balance for the connected public address on the selected network.
  • Links to the official explorer pages for independent checking.

What is not live yet

  • No bridge or cross-chain backing between the two ZV contracts.
  • No staking, rewards, governance, swap interface, or token-gated utility.
  • No independent smart-contract audit or liquidity verification implied.

Next gates: review BNB proxy implementation/owner privileges, verify actual pool liquidity and market activity, then define and security-review one concrete utility before adding transactions.
